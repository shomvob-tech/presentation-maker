On the mobile number field

A required field, filled without using anyone's personal number.

The mobile number field is part of how every employee account logs into the system, so it can't be left empty. What it holds is up to Terraprime — instead of a personal number, each employee gets a structured placeholder built from a fixed prefix and their employee ID, so the field is populated without a single real contact number ever being entered.

The placeholder format
  • Fixed prefix 01200000, common to every Terraprime employee
  • Last 3 digits are the employee's own ID number
  • Together they form one valid, unique login number per employee
  • HR assigns it once at onboarding — no personal number is ever collected

Data protection & compliance
  • Data encrypted in transit and at rest across the platform
  • Role-based access — staff, department heads, and admins each see only what their role permits
  • Platform practices built to align with international data protection standards and applicable law
  • Ongoing security review as a standard part of platform operations

In practice: employees still log in the way the system expects — a mobile number and their credentials — it's just never their own. HR controls the ID-to-number mapping internally, and it can be changed at any time without touching how Attendance, Leave, or Device Integration function day to day.
