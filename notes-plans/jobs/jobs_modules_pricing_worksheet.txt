HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This is the Jobs branch, NOT the HRIS/HR-software product. Separate services,
  separate pricing logic, separate buyer.

  Every module below has these parts:

     WHAT IT DELIVERS   Plain description. Edit freely.
     ON RECORD          What our existing documents say, with the source tag.
                        DO NOT EDIT -- this is evidence.
     CONFLICTS          Where the documents disagree with each other.
     DECIDE             Open questions. Tick when settled.
     >> FINAL           Empty. Write the agreed answer here.

  Only the >> FINAL blocks and the DECIDE ticks should change during review.
  When every >> FINAL block is filled, this file becomes the rate card and we
  can build the proposal generator off it.

X1 -- VAT, AIT & BILLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ON RECORD
    [C][D]  "15% VAT to be included."   (footnote to the head-hunting table)
    [B]     "a 5% VAT will be excluded from the total billing"  (stated twice)
    [E]     "VAT and AIT will be applicable as per prevailing Government
             regulations."  -- no rate given
    HRIS side, for comparison (notes-plans/proposal_format.md):
            "5% VAT will be added on the total invoice amount."

CONFLICTS
    1. 5% vs 15%. A 3x difference on every invoice. HR software and
       recruitment services may genuinely attract different VAT rates -- but
       nobody has written down which applies to which, and [B] applies 5% to
       recruitment services.
    2. The word "excluded" in [B] is almost certainly wrong. "VAT will be
       excluded from the total billing" reads to a client as "you do not pay
       VAT". [C][D] say "included", which reads as the opposite -- that the
       quoted price already contains VAT. Both are sloppy. The correct
       phrasing is either "exclusive of VAT" or "VAT will be added".
    3. AIT appears ONLY in the signed agreement. No deck mentions it, so no
       client sees it coming.
