2.Service Availability

  • System Uptime — the SaaS HRIS system will be available 99.5% of the time per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance periods.
  • Scheduled Maintenance — performed during non-business hours; the Provider will notify the Client at least 48 hours in advance.

3.Performance Metrics

Response Time

  • Critical issues (e.g., system downtime) will be addressed within 2 hours.
  • Non-critical issues (e.g., minor bugs) will be addressed within 24 hours.

Resolution Time

  • For critical issues, the system will be restored to normal functionality within 12 hours.
  • For non-critical issues, resolution will occur within 48 hours.

4.Data Security & Compliance

  • Data Encryption — all sensitive data stored in the system will be encrypted both in transit and at rest.
  • Data Backup — regular backups of the Client's data will be performed and retained for up to 30 days.
  • Compliance — the system will comply with applicable data protection laws, including GDPR where applicable.
  • Hosting — the Provider's solution is hosted on AWS under standard security protocols; no on-premises servers are used for data storage.
  • Access Control — the Provider will not access Client accounts without the Client's permission.
  • Data Retention on Exit — data is deleted per the Client's instruction, or automatically after 6 months if no instruction is given.
  • Backup Schedule — scheduled backups occur twice daily for data recovery purposes.
  • Data Portability — following discontinuation of service, the Client may request a data transfer within 2 months, free of charge.
  • Liability — the Client may pursue legal action against the Provider if data is lost as a result of the Provider's operational negligence.
  • Transport Security — data transfers are secured using SSL/TLS (HTTPS) with a DV certificate reissued every 90 days; passwords are protected using the Provider's proprietary encryption mechanism.

5.Support Services

Helpdesk Support — available 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Saturday to Thursday.

Support Response Time

  • Critical issues will be responded to within 2 hours.
  • Non-critical issues will be responded to within 1 business day.

Support Channels — email, phone, or an online ticketing system.

6.Client Responsibilities

  • User Training — the Client will ensure relevant personnel are trained on the SaaS HRIS system.
  • User Access — the Client is responsible for managing user access so that only authorized personnel reach sensitive HR data.
  • Infrastructure Requirements — the Client must maintain an internet connection and any necessary hardware.
