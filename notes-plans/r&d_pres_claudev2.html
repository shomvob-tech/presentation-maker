Attendance

Built for teams like yours — field staff and onsite employees tracked in one place, not two.

KEY FEATURES
  • Mobile check-in / check-out with a live attendance overview
  • Remote attendance history and attendance location mapping
  • Work-from-home requests — pending, current, upcoming & history
  • Forget-attendance and late-waiver requests with approval flow
  • Break time tracking and waiver management
  • Attendance reports by date range or by month
HOW THIS
HELPS

One accurate attendance picture across remote and onsite employees, with self-service requests and approvals on mobile.

REQUIREMENT 02 / 06

Leave Management

A clear, policy-driven system, so leave stops being a manual back-and-forth over messages.

KEY FEATURES
  • Configurable leave policies and leave types for your company
  • Holiday calendar and special weekly leave settings
  • Bonus leave configuration and attendance bonus ledger
  • Employees apply for leave directly from the mobile app
  • Approval Center for pending, current & upcoming requests
  • Leave history reporting for audits and record-keeping
HOW THIS
HELPS

Employees see their balances and apply on the go; managers approve in seconds — no more manual tracking on spreadsheets.

REQUIREMENT 03 / 06 TOP PRIORITY

Payroll

Payroll is your top priority. Every cycle is built to be accurate, fast, and easy to reconcile.

KEY FEATURES
  • Payroll dashboard with drafts, outstanding salary & full history
  • Payslip history, accessible to employees on demand
  • Bonus dashboard, off-cycle bonus, and bonus payslip history
  • Configurable bonus types & policies
  • Reports exportable in the exact layout your finance team needs — employee name, bank account name, payable amount, and salary month
  • Mobile quick-access to payroll for employees
HOW THIS
HELPS

One dashboard drives the full payroll cycle, with reports already formatted for your bank disbursement process.
